ANCIENT WISDOM

Introduction

The Indian Knowledge System (IKS) represents India's rich intellectual and cultural heritage. Traditionally, knowledge was organised into different branches known as Vidyāsthānas.


The Four Upāṅgas are important supplementary disciplines that help understand, interpret and apply traditional knowledge. They include Nyāya, Mīmāṃsā, Itihāsa-Purāṇa and Dharmaśāstra.

THE FOUR PILLARS

Four disciplines that represent different dimensions of Indian intellectual tradition.

01 / LOGIC
⚖️

Nyāya

न्याय

Nyāya is the school of Indian philosophy concerned with logic, reasoning and valid knowledge. It teaches systematic thinking and methods of arriving at reliable conclusions.

Key Concepts

• Logical reasoning
• Critical thinking
• Methods of knowledge
• Debate and argumentation

02 / INTERPRETATION
📜

Mīmāṃsā

मीमांसा

Mīmāṃsā focuses on the interpretation of Vedic texts and understanding their meaning. It examines language, rituals and the principles used to interpret sacred instructions.

Key Concepts

• Vedic interpretation
• Analysis of language
• Ritual philosophy
• Understanding textual meaning

03 / HISTORY & CULTURE
🏛️

Itihāsa–Purāṇa

इतिहास–पुराण

Itihāsa and Purāṇa preserve traditional narratives, cultural memories, philosophical teachings and accounts of ancient Indian traditions.

Key Concepts

• Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata
• Purāṇic literature
• Cultural heritage
• Traditional knowledge and values

04 / ETHICS & SOCIETY
⚖️

Dharmaśāstra

धर्मशास्त्र

Dharmaśāstra deals with Dharma, duties, ethical principles and social conduct. It discusses responsibilities of individuals and the organisation of society in traditional Indian thought.

Key Concepts

• Ethics and morality
• Duties and responsibilities
• Social conduct
• Traditional legal principles

KNOWLEDGE & WISDOM

Why Are the Four Upāṅgas Important?

The Four Upāṅgas provide different ways of understanding knowledge and human life. Nyāya develops reasoning, Mīmāṃsā develops interpretation, Itihāsa-Purāṇa preserves cultural knowledge, and Dharmaśāstra discusses ethical and social responsibilities.
