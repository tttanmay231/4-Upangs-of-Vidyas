It covers diverse fields including philosophy, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, literature, architecture, language and ethics. It reflects the intellectual contributions of ancient Indian scholars and traditions.

INDIAN KNOWLEDGE SYSTEM

Introduction to Vidyās

Discover the traditional classification of knowledge in ancient India.

What are Vidyās?

Vidyās refer to different branches of knowledge and learning in the Indian Knowledge System. Ancient Indian scholars organised knowledge into various disciplines to develop intellectual understanding, spiritual wisdom and practical skills.

Traditionally, fourteen major branches of knowledge are known as the Chaturdaśa Vidyāsthānas. They consist of four Vedas, six Vedāṅgas and four Upāṅgas.

THE THREE GROUPS

Classification of Vidyās

The fourteen traditional disciplines are divided into three groups.

📜
01 / FOUR BRANCHES

Four Vedas

चत्वारो वेदाः

The Vedas are foundational sacred texts of Indian tradition. They contain hymns, prayers, ritual knowledge and philosophical teachings.

The Four Vedas

1. Rigveda – Hymns and prayers.
2. Yajurveda – Ritual formulas and procedures.
3. Samaveda – Melodies and chants.
4. Atharvaveda – Hymns, prayers and everyday traditions.

📚
02 / SIX BRANCHES

Six Vedāṅgas

षड् वेदाङ्गानि

Vedāṅgas are six supplementary disciplines that help in understanding, preserving and correctly interpreting the Vedas.

The Six Vedāṅgas

1. Śikṣā – Phonetics and pronunciation.
2. Kalpa – Ritual procedures.
3. Vyākaraṇa – Grammar.
4. Nirukta – Etymology and explanation of words.
5. Chandas – Vedic metres.
6. Jyotiṣa – Astronomy and calendrical calculations.

⚖️
03 / FOUR BRANCHES

Four Upāṅgas

चत्वार उपाङ्गानि

Upāṅgas are supplementary disciplines that contribute to understanding philosophy, logic, traditional narratives and ethical principles.

The Four Upāṅgas

1. Purāṇa – Traditional narratives and cultural knowledge.
2. Nyāya – Logic and reasoning.
3. Mīmāṃsā – Interpretation of Vedic texts.
4. Dharmaśāstra – Duties, ethics and social conduct.

01 — CULTURAL KNOWLEDGE
📜

Purāṇa

पुराण
Description

Purāṇas are traditional Indian texts that preserve ancient narratives, cosmology, genealogies, cultural memories and philosophical teachings. They explain religious traditions, creation stories and the history of dynasties.

Key Concepts
  • Ancient narratives and traditions
  • Cosmology and creation accounts
  • Dynasties and genealogies
Examples

1. Viṣṇu Purāṇa – Discusses creation, cosmology and dynasties.
2. Bhāgavata Purāṇa – Focuses on devotion and the life of Krishna.
3. Śiva Purāṇa – Describes traditions and stories associated with Shiva.

Contribution

• Preserves India's cultural and religious heritage.
• Transmits traditional knowledge through stories.
• Provides philosophical and moral teachings.
• Helps understand ancient Indian traditions and beliefs.

02 — LOGIC & REASONING
⚖️

Nyāya

न्याय
Description

Nyāya is a school of Indian philosophy concerned with logic, reasoning and valid knowledge. It develops systematic methods of thinking, inference, debate and arriving at reliable conclusions.

Key Concepts
  • Logical reasoning
  • Critical thinking
  • Valid sources of knowledge
Examples

1. Nyāya Sūtras – Traditionally attributed to Akṣapāda Gautama.
2. Pratyakṣa – Knowledge through perception.
3. Anumāna – Knowledge through inference.

Contribution

• Develops logical and analytical thinking.
• Encourages questioning and critical examination.
• Establishes methods of valid reasoning.
• Improves debate and problem-solving skills.

03 — INTERPRETATION
📚

Mīmāṃsā

मीमांसा
Description

Mīmāṃsā is a philosophical discipline that focuses on the interpretation of Vedic texts. It examines the meaning of language, Vedic instructions, rituals and the principles used to understand sacred texts.

Key Concepts
  • Vedic interpretation
  • Language and meaning
  • Ritual philosophy
Examples

1. Mīmāṃsā Sūtras – Traditionally attributed to Jaimini.
2. Śabara Bhāṣya – An important commentary on the Mīmāṃsā Sūtras.
3. Kumārila Bhaṭṭa – An influential Mīmāṃsā philosopher.

Contribution

• Develops systematic methods of textual interpretation.
• Explores the meaning of Vedic language.
• Provides principles for understanding instructions and duties.
• Contributes to Indian philosophy and linguistic analysis.

04 — ETHICS & SOCIETY
🏛️

Dharmaśāstra

धर्मशास्त्र
Description

Dharmaśāstra is a traditional branch of Indian literature dealing with Dharma, ethical principles, duties, responsibilities and social conduct. It discusses individual responsibilities and traditional legal frameworks.

Key Concepts
  • Ethics and morality
  • Duties and responsibilities
  • Social conduct and traditions
Examples

1. Manusmṛti – A traditional text discussing Dharma and social duties.
2. Yājñavalkya Smṛti – Discusses conduct, legal matters and responsibilities.
3. Nārada Smṛti – Focuses particularly on traditional legal procedures.

Contribution

• Explores ethical and moral principles.
• Discusses duties and responsibilities.
• Documents traditional social and legal frameworks.
• Contributes to the study of India's legal and social history.
