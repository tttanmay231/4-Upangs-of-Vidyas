THE FOUNDATION

What Are the Four Upāṅgas?

Understanding the supplementary branches of traditional Indian knowledge.

The Indian Knowledge System represents a rich heritage of intellectual, philosophical and cultural traditions. Traditionally, knowledge was organised into fourteen Vidyāsthānas — four Vedas, six Vedāṅgas and four Upāṅgas.


The four Upāṅgas are Purāṇa, Nyāya, Mīmāṃsā and Dharmaśāstra. Each discipline contributes a distinct way of understanding knowledge, society and human life.

01 — CULTURAL KNOWLEDGE
📜

Purāṇa

पुराण

Purāṇas preserve traditional narratives, cosmology, genealogies, cultural memories and philosophical teachings.

  • Ancient narratives and traditions
  • Cosmology and creation accounts
  • Dynasties and genealogies
Examples

Viṣṇu Purāṇa, Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Śiva Purāṇa and other traditional texts.


Contribution

Preserves cultural heritage, traditional knowledge and moral teachings through stories and narratives.

02 — LOGIC & REASONING
⚖️

Nyāya

न्याय

Nyāya is the philosophical discipline of logic, critical reasoning and the systematic examination of valid knowledge.

  • Logical reasoning
  • Critical thinking
  • Valid sources of knowledge
Associated thinker

Akṣapāda Gautama, traditionally associated with the Nyāya Sūtras.


Contribution

Develops methods of argument, inference, debate and logical analysis.

03 — INTERPRETATION
📚

Mīmāṃsā

मीमांसा

Mīmāṃsā examines the meaning and interpretation of Vedic texts, particularly their language, instructions and ritual significance.

  • Vedic interpretation
  • Language and meaning
  • Ritual philosophy
Associated thinker

Jaimini, traditionally credited with the Mīmāṃsā Sūtras.


Contribution

Develops systematic methods for understanding texts, interpreting instructions and examining duties.

04 — ETHICS & SOCIETY
🏛️

Dharmaśāstra

धर्मशास्त्र

Dharmaśāstra deals with Dharma, ethical principles, duties, responsibilities and traditional social and legal frameworks.

  • Ethics and morality
  • Duties and responsibilities
  • Social conduct and traditions
Examples

Manusmṛti, Yājñavalkya Smṛti and other Dharmaśāstra texts.


Contribution

Explores traditional ideas about individual duties, social responsibilities and ethical conduct.

AT A GLANCE

Understanding the Differences

Each Upāṅga has a unique role in the knowledge tradition.

📜

Purāṇa

Preserves traditional narratives and cultural knowledge.

⚖️

Nyāya

Develops logic, reasoning and critical thinking.

📚

Mīmāṃsā

Examines interpretation and meaning of Vedic texts.

🏛️

Dharmaśāstra

Discusses duties, ethics and social conduct.

Knowledge • Wisdom • Tradition

The four Upāṅgas demonstrate the breadth of Indian intellectual tradition — preserving knowledge, questioning ideas, interpreting texts and exploring ethical responsibilities.
